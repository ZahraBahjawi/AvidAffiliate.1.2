Appendix: Uplift Calculation Methodology

The Estimated Revenue Uplift is a projection based on actionable opportunities. It is not a guarantee but a data‑driven estimate of potential.

  • Broken links: recoverable revenue modeled from lost clicks and baseline EPC (or comparable working links).
  • Program swaps: delta estimated from current EPC vs. recommended EPC applied to eligible clicks.
