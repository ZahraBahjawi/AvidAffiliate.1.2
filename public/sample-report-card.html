Appendix: Methodology & Impact Estimation

The **Estimated Revenue Uplift** is a projection, not a guarantee, based on actionable opportunities identified during our scan. We estimate uplift based on your site's existing monetized links and traffic (if provided), combined with industry benchmarks and commission rate analysis across our proprietary database of 35,000+ affiliate programs.

For this free report card, the percentage uplift is a simplified calculation to provide a directional estimate. It's derived by summing the number of broken and unmonetized affiliate links and dividing that total by the number of existing, working affiliate links. This method assumes, for modeling purposes, that each link holds a similar potential value.

Example Calculation: A site with 1,251 working affiliate links is found to have 98 broken affiliate links and 688 unmonetized opportunities. The uplift is calculated as: (98 + 688) / 1,251 = ~63%.
